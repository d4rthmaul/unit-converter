What this site uses, and how

Names, only to identify things. Franchise names appear only so you know which universe a unit comes from. There are no logos, artwork, screenshots or other images from any of these works.

Numbers, which are facts. A Galleon is worth 493 Knuts and 50 gold pieces weigh a pound because the source material says so. The site reports those figures and converts them.

Descriptions written for this site. Each unit's note is a short summary written for this site, sometimes with a brief quotation, and every one links to the source it came from. Those sources, including the fan wikis, belong to their authors.

Real-but-humorous units, like the smoot and the beard-second, belong to the people and traditions that invented them. Standard units follow official definitions, mostly from NIST.

Something wrong, or yours?

If you own any of this material and want something changed or removed, or you've spotted a wrong number, please open an issue on GitHub. I'll take care of it promptly.
