Nerdy Unit Converter

Every unit, what it's worth, and where the number comes from.

Welcome to the Dimensional Analysis Machine. (That's fancy talk for "unit converter".) Alongside meters, kilograms and liters, it converts units from your favorite sci-fi and fantasy universes: D&D gold pieces, Galleons, warp factors, Elvish long years and even midi-chlorians.

Standard units follow official definitions, mostly from NIST. Fictional units cite canon sources, or fan wikis that quote canon. Where a universe never pins a number down, the note says what assumption was made.
