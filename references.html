Nerdy Unit Converter

Units & Sources

Welcome to the Dimensional Analysis Machine! (That's fancy talk for "unit converter".) Alongside standard units like meters, kilograms and liters, it converts units from your favorite sci-fi and fantasy universes: D&D gold pieces, Galleons, warp factors, Elvish long years and even midi-chlorians.

This page lists every unit, what it's worth and where the number comes from. Standard units follow official definitions (mostly NIST). Fictional units cite canon sources, or fan wikis that quote canon. Where a universe never pins a number down, the note says what assumption was made.
